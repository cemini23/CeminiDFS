### The one transferable note

The strongest paper in this wave found that **92.6% of runs passing every final check still had a process deviation.** The DFS analogue: a slate can produce a valid lineup that violates a process constraint — exposure cap, stacking rule, late-swap window — and still look fine on the final score. If we check a slate, check the constraints too, not just the projected points.
