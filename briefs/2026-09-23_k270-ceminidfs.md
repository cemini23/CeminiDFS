## Body

### 1. Option market making and rebate design (arXiv:2609.26606)

The paper solves a nested optimisation problem:

- **Market maker** trades multiple European calls in a local-stochastic volatility market. Make is a continuous control; take is an impulse control. The objective maximises portfolio value plus cumulative rebate revenue, under a penalty on residual portfolio delta and vega.
- **Exchange** sets fee rebates to attract liquidity. The paper proposes a three-step rebate design scheme with flexibility for a specific liquidity target.
- **Technique:** Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman quasi-variational inequality. Numerical results validate the scheme.
